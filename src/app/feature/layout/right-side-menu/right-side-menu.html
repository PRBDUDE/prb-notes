Right Side menu Layout displays the links to dig deeper into the menu item selected on the left side.

Left and Right Side menu Layout displays the menu on the Left and Right side of the page. The Left menu is used like a submenu of the top menu-bar. The Right menu is used like a submenu of the Left menu. In this application (PRB Notes), I place the primary link at the top of the menu to show which top-menu item was selected to get to this page.
